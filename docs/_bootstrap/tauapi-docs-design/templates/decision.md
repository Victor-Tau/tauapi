# Decision title

## Context

Describe the concrete problem, relevant constraints, and evidence. Distinguish
observed behavior from assumptions.

## Decision

State the chosen approach and its scope. Record who or what authorized the
decision when that matters; do not invent approval.

## Alternatives and consequences

Explain material tradeoffs, compatibility, operational effects, and recurring
maintenance cost. Keep rejected alternatives concise and specific.

## Implementation and verification

Link the implementation/change record, affected contracts, and acceptance
evidence. Use a separate implementation status if an accepted decision has not
yet shipped. Accepted intent does not prove deployed behavior.

## Supersession

Link the previous decision and the reason it is replaced. Preserve historical
context rather than rewriting old decisions as if the new choice always existed.
